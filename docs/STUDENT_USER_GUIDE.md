## 1. Accessing the Student Portal

### 1.1 Web Portal Link
Open your smartphone or computer browser and navigate to:
```
https://portal.mipsedu.in   (or your college's student results portal link)
```

> [!NOTE]
> **SCREENSHOT PLACEHOLDER**: *[Screenshot: Student Portal Mobile landing page showing college crest, "Student Examination & Academic Portal", and login input fields]*

### 1.2 Installing the Mobile Web App (PWA) on Your Home Screen
For instant 1-tap access on your phone without opening a browser:
- **Android (Chrome)**: Tap the three dots (⋮) at top-right > Tap **"Install app"** (or **"Add to Home screen"**).
- **iPhone / iOS (Safari)**: Tap the Share button (⎋ with arrow) at bottom > Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.

An app icon labeled **MIPS Portal** will appear on your phone's home screen.

## 2. First-Time Account Activation & Password Setup

If you are logging in for the first time:

> [!NOTE]
> **SCREENSHOT PLACEHOLDER**: *[Screenshot: First Login Activation screen showing Registration Number input, Date of Birth calendar picker (YYYY-MM-DD), and Create New Password inputs]*

### 2.1 First-Time Login Steps
1. On the login screen, enter your official **University / College Registration Number** (e.g., `2023MLT042`).
2. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the format `YYYY-MM-DD` (e.g., `2004-08-15`).
3. Click **Log In**.
4. The system validates your registration and prompts you to **Set Your Permanent Password**.

### 2.2 Password Security Rules
To protect your academic transcripts and financial records:
- Must be at least **8 characters** long.
- Must **not** contain your registration number.
- Must **not** contain your date of birth or birth year.
- Must include a combination of letters, numbers, and symbols (e.g., `Mips@Student#2026`).
5. Click **Save Password & Access Portal**. You will be logged into your student dashboard.

## 3. Viewing Examination Results & Downloading Marksheets

Navigate to the **Results** tab in the top navigation bar.

> [!NOTE]
> **SCREENSHOT PLACEHOLDER**: *[Screenshot: Results Tab displaying Examination Card, overall GPA/Percentage badge (82.4% - First Class with Distinction), Pass/Fail banner, and Download Official Marksheet PDF button]*

### 3.1 Reviewing Examination Transcripts
- Select the examination from the dropdown (e.g., *First Year Final Examination - March 2026*).
- **Summary Header**:
  - Overall Result: **PASS** (Green) or **FAIL** (Red).
  - Aggregate Percentage: e.g., `84.5%`.
  - Grade Band: e.g., **A+ (Distinction)**.
- **Subject-by-Subject Breakdown**:
  - Subject Name & Code (e.g., *Biochemistry & Clinical Pathology [BCP-101]*).
  - Maximum Marks (e.g., `100`).
  - Minimum Pass Marks (e.g., `40`).
  - Marks Obtained (e.g., `82`).
  - Subject Grade & Status (**PASS** / **FAIL** / **ABSENT**).

### 3.2 Downloading Official PDF Marksheet
1. Click the blue **Download Marksheet (PDF)** button.
2. The portal compiles an official verified document formatted for standard A4 printing.
3. **Security Elements on Marksheet**:
   - Official College Letterhead, Address, and Government Affiliation.
   - Student Passport Photograph and Registration Barcode.
   - Complete Subject Ledger, Total Marks, and Cumulative Percentage.
   - **Verification QR Code**: Scannable by prospective employers, universities, or banks to verify document authenticity online.
   - Official Seal & Controller of Examinations Signature space.

## 4. Fee Ledger & Payment Tracking

Navigate to the **Fees** tab.

> [!NOTE]
> **SCREENSHOT PLACEHOLDER**: *[Screenshot: Fees Tab displaying Total Course Fee card (₹50,000), Amount Paid card (₹35,000), Outstanding Balance card (₹15,000 in Amber), and Payment Receipts history table]*

### 4.1 Financial Ledger Cards
- **Total Course Fee**: Total institutional fee obligation billed for your enrolled program.
- **Total Amount Paid**: Sum of all confirmed installments received by the college office.
- **Outstanding Balance**: Remaining pending amount due.

### 4.2 Installment Schedule & Due Dates
- Displays each term or semester fee installment, amount in Rupees, and due date.
- Status chips indicate whether each installment is `Paid` (Green), `Partially Paid` (Amber), or `Overdue` (Red).

### 4.3 Payment Receipts & History
- Table lists every historical payment recorded at the cash counter or bank:
  - Official Receipt Number (e.g., `REC-2026-0042`).
  - Payment Date.
  - Payment Mode (*Cash*, *UPI / GooglePay*, *Bank Transfer*).
  - Amount Paid (₹).
- Click **Download Slip** to reprint any past payment receipt slip for your records.

## 5. Attendance Monitoring, Warning Bands & Recovery

Navigate to the **Attendance** tab.

> [!NOTE]
> **SCREENSHOT PLACEHOLDER**: *[Screenshot: Attendance Tab displaying overall 86.5% gauge meter, 75% mandatory benchmark marker, Morning vs Evening stats, and monthly interactive calendar view]*

### 5.1 Mandatory Minimum Attendance (75%)
State paramedical board and college regulations require a minimum of **75% attendance** to be eligible to appear for university examinations.

### 5.2 Attendance Status Bands & Warning Banners
The portal continuously calculates your percentage and displays your status:

#### A. Good Standing (`ok` Band — $> 80\%$)
- Displayed with a green badge: **Attendance On Track**.
- You comfortably exceed the minimum threshold.

#### B. Low Attendance Reminder (`low` Band — $75\% \text{ to } 80\%$)
- Displayed with an amber warning banner:
  > *"Low Attendance Reminder: Your attendance is currently 76.2%. You can miss only 1 more session before falling below the 75% minimum threshold."*
- Tells you exactly how many sessions you can miss before facing examination shortage.

#### C. Shortage Warning (`shortage` Band — $< 75\%$)
- Displayed with a red alert banner:
  > *"Attendance Below Minimum: Your overall attendance is 68.5%, which is below the mandatory 75% threshold. You must attend 7 sessions in a row to recover to 75%."*
- Provides an automated recovery calculator indicating how many consecutive classes you must attend to regain exam eligibility.

### 5.3 Detailed Session Breakdown
- **Sessions Held**: Total lecture and lab sessions conducted to date.
- **Present**: Sessions attended on time.
- **Late**: Arrived after roll call (calculated with 0.5 weight toward attendance).
- **Absent**: Unexcused missed classes.
- **Approved Leave**: Officially approved medical or institutional leave (excluded from denominator so your percentage is not penalized).

### 5.4 Morning vs. Evening Statistics
Compare your attendance across the academic day:
- **Morning Slot** (09:00 AM – 12:30 PM): Attendance % and session counts.
- **Evening Slot** (01:30 PM – 04:30 PM): Attendance % and session counts.

### 5.5 Monthly Interactive Attendance Calendar
- Visual calendar grid of the selected month.
- Each date displays colored dots indicating your attendance:
  - 🟢 Green dot = Present
  - 🟡 Amber dot = Late
  - 🔴 Red dot = Absent
  - ⚪ Grey dot = Approved Leave

### 5.6 Dual-Enrollment Notice (Primary vs. Skill Course)
If you are enrolled in a secondary Skill Course (e.g., *Phlebotomy Certification*), a purple banner explains:
> *"Dual Enrollment: Attendance tracking is not applicable to skill courses. Showing attendance records for primary course only."*

## 6. Campus Announcements & Web Push Notifications

> [!NOTE]
> **SCREENSHOT PLACEHOLDER**: *[Screenshot: Push Notification permission prompt modal and Announcements feed showing Exam Schedule announcement with Urgent tag]*

### 6.1 Enabling Push Notifications
- When prompted, click **Allow Notifications**.
- You will receive instant alerts on your phone screen whenever:
  - An examination result or marksheet is published.
  - An urgent holiday or timetable change is announced.
  - A fee due date or payment receipt is generated.

### 6.2 Reviewing Campus Announcements
- Click the **Bell Icon** on the top header to view recent institutional circulars.
- Announcements are tagged by category (*Academic*, *Exams*, *Holiday*, *Fees*, *Urgent*).

## 7. Signing Out Safely

> [!NOTE]
> **SCREENSHOT PLACEHOLDER**: *[Screenshot: Top header showing Log Out icon button and Confirm Sign Out dialog]*

1. Click the **Log Out** button on the top right of the screen.
2. In the confirmation dialog, click **Sign Out**.
3. **Shared Devices Notice**: Always log out when accessing the portal on shared library computers, friends' phones, or cyber cafe PCs to protect your personal academic records.
